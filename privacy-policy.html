Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED · AUGUST 2026

This page explains what information aroop.netlify.app collects when you visit, why, and how you can ask for it to be removed. This site is run by Abu Arefin Aroop as an individual portfolio — there's no company or third-party data broker involved.

What's collected automatically

When you browse the site, a lightweight analytics script records:

  • Which sections of the site you open (e.g. VAPT, AI/ML, Research) and roughly how long you spend on each
  • Which buttons/links you click (e.g. WhatsApp, email, certificate links)
  • Your approximate location (country-level, derived from IP address) and device type
  • The page that referred you here, and basic browser/screen info
  • Your IP address, for spam/abuse prevention

This is stored to understand how the site is used and improve it — not sold, shared, or used for advertising. It's stored in Netlify's infrastructure and is only accessible to the site owner via a password-protected dashboard.

What's collected if you use the chat assistant

If you chat with the on-site AI assistant, your messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a response. If you share your name, email, or project interest during that conversation, it may be logged as a "lead" so Aroop can follow up with you directly. Don't share sensitive information (passwords, financial details, etc.) in the chat — it's a portfolio inquiry tool, not a secure channel.

What's collected if you use the contact form

The contact form opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message with what you typed — the message content goes to WhatsApp, not to this site's servers, since the form doesn't submit anywhere itself.

How long data is kept

Analytics data is retained to understand trends over time; there's no automatic expiry currently configured. If you'd like your data deleted, contact the email below and it will be removed.

Your choices

  • You can browse with your browser's tracking-protection or ad-blocking features on — the analytics script fails silently if blocked, and the rest of the site still works normally.
  • You can request a copy of, or deletion of, any data associated with your session by emailing aroop.cse@gmail.com.

Third parties involved

  • Netlify — hosting, and stores analytics data (Netlify Blobs)
  • Anthropic — processes chat messages you send to the AI assistant, per Anthropic's privacy policy
  • WhatsApp/Meta — if you use the WhatsApp contact option, per WhatsApp's own privacy policy
  • Google Fonts — for webfonts (loads a font file from Google's CDN)

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: aroop.cse@gmail.com
